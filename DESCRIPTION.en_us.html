Leraw is a sans-serif typeface designed for writing Kurdish in both Sorani (Arabic script) and Kurmanji (Latin script). It is based on Rubik by Hubert & Fischer, extending the family with the distinct Sorani letters (ڕ, ڵ, ۆ, ێ, ە, ھ) and featuring matching 12° slanted Arabic italics.

The family spans seven weights from Light (300) to Black (900), each with true matching italics, and is available as a variable font along the weight axis.

To contribute, see github.com/iolitetech/Leraw.
